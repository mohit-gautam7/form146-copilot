# 004 Quotes are checked as exact substrings
**Context.** Models invent plausible quotes. An auditor must be able to find every cited sentence in the source.
**Decision.** Every quote must appear verbatim (after normalising case, whitespace and curly quotes) in the contract, invoice or treaty. One invented quote voids that model's whole vote.
**Why.** Fuzzy matching would accept paraphrases, which is exactly what the check exists to stop. Voiding the whole vote, not just the quote, makes fabrication costly: a model that invents evidence is not evidence.
**Trade-off.** Models that paraphrase honestly lose their vote. In practice that shows up as more abstentions, which is the safe direction.
