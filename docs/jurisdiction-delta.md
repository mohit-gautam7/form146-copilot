# What changes for another jurisdiction
The three-lane split carries over unchanged. What changes is the rule data and some engine branches.
**United States (paying a foreign vendor).** The payer collects Form W-8BEN-E instead of a TRC and Form 10F, withholds under chapter 3 at 30% or the treaty rate, and reports on Form 1042-S. There is no CA certificate before payment; the withholding agent is liable. The AI lane's question changes from "FTS or business profits?" to "US-source FDAP income or not?", where services performed outside the US are generally not US-source. The engine needs a sourcing step that India's place-of-payer rule does not.
**United Kingdom.** Withholding applies mainly to royalties and interest, not services, so most of India's "make available" problem disappears. A treaty rate needs HMRC clearance or the DT scheme. The AI lane shrinks to "is this a royalty?".
**Another Indian treaty.** Data only: add the article text to fetch_treaties.py, add rate rows with source quotes, set the treaty flags (make-available test, managerial services, equipment in the royalty definition). No code change unless the treaty has a new structure, such as a separate article for technical services.
